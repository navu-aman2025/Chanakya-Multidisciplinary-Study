# Sociological Contributions

Chanakya’s sociological perspective addresses social order, class structure, community welfare, and ethical conduct. His emphasis on discipline, education, and social responsibility reflects an early understanding of societal dynamics.

Key sociological themes:
- Social stratification and occupational roles
- Education and moral development
- Community welfare and public duties
- Law, justice, and social regulation
- Social cohesion and stability

His sociological insights reveal a holistic approach to maintaining societal harmony and collective progress.

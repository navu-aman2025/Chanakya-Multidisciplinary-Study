# List of Figures

This section provides a consolidated list of all diagrams included in the study, along with their corresponding figure numbers and titles. These visuals support the multidisciplinary analysis of Chanakya’s contributions to economics, political science, sociology, governance, and comparative philosophy.

---

## **Figure 1 — Saptanga Theory**
A visual representation of Chanakya’s seven limbs of the state: Swami, Amatya, Janapada, Durga, Kosha, Danda, and Mitra.

## **Figure 2 — Mandala Theory**
A diagram illustrating Chanakya’s geopolitical model of concentric circles of allies, enemies, neutral states, and middle powers surrounding the central king (Vijigishu).

## **Figure 3 — Economic System of the State**
A structured diagram showing revenue sources, taxation models, market regulation, and welfare measures within Chanakya’s economic framework.

## **Figure 4 — Governance Flowchart**
A hierarchical flowchart depicting Chanakya’s administrative structure, from the King to Ministers, Departments, Officers, and Citizens.

## **Figure 5 — Comparative Analysis Chart**
A comparative visual chart analyzing philosophical contributions of Chanakya, Plato, Aristotle, Manu, and Shukra across ethics, governance, economics, social structure, and diplomacy.

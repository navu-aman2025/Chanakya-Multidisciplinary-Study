# Glossary of Key Terms

This glossary provides definitions of important Sanskrit, political, economic, and philosophical terms used throughout the study. It serves as a quick reference for readers unfamiliar with classical Indian terminology or Chanakya’s conceptual framework.

---

## **A**

### **Amatya**
Ministers or high-ranking officials responsible for administration, policy execution, and advising the king.

### **Arthashastra**
Chanakya’s foundational treatise on economics, politics, governance, diplomacy, and statecraft.

---

## **D**

### **Danda**
The principle of punishment, coercive power, and enforcement used to maintain law, order, and discipline.

### **Durga**
Fort or fortified city; represents military defense and strategic protection of the state.

---

## **J**

### **Janapada**
Territory or land of the people; includes population, agriculture, resources, and settlements forming the economic base of the state.

---

## **K**

### **Kosha**
Treasury or financial reserves of the state; includes revenue, taxation, and wealth management.

### **Kshatra**
The warrior class or military authority responsible for defense and security.

---

## **M**

### **Mitra**
Ally or friendly state; a key component in Chanakya’s Mandala Theory of diplomacy.

### **Mandala Theory**
Chanakya’s geopolitical model describing concentric circles of allies, enemies, neutral states, and middle powers around the central king (Vijigishu).

---

## **N**

### **Niti**
Policy, ethics, or guiding principles for governance and personal conduct.

---

## **R**

### **Rajya**
The state; includes territory, people, sovereignty, administration, and institutions.

### **Raja**
The king or sovereign authority responsible for leadership, governance, and protection of the state.

---

## **S**

### **Saptanga Theory**
Chanakya’s model of the seven limbs of the state: Swami, Amatya, Janapada, Durga, Kosha, Danda, and Mitra.

### **Shukraniti**
A classical Indian text on governance, ethics, administration, and leadership attributed to Shukra Acharya.

### **Swami**
The ruler or king; central authority in the Saptanga framework.

---

## **V**

### **Vijigishu**
The aspiring conqueror or central king in the Mandala Theory.

### **Vyavahara**
Judicial procedures, legal disputes, and systems of justice.

---

## **Y**

### **Yukti**
Strategic reasoning, logic, and practical intelligence used in decision-making.

---

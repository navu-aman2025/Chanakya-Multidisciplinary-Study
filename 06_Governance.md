# Governance & Administration

Chanakya’s administrative model is one of the most detailed in ancient literature. It covers bureaucracy, public institutions, law enforcement, infrastructure, and accountability mechanisms.

Core governance principles:
- Efficient bureaucracy and trained officials
- Anti-corruption measures and audits
- Public infrastructure and resource management
- Law enforcement and judicial systems
- Welfare policies and disaster management

His governance framework aligns closely with modern public administration theories.

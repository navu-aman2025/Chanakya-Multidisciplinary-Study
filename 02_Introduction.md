# Introduction

Chanakya, also known as Kautilya or Vishnugupta, remains one of the most influential thinkers in Indian intellectual history. His treatise, the Arthashastra, is a foundational text on governance, economics, diplomacy, and statecraft. This research project explores Chanakya’s multidisciplinary impact, situating his ideas within historical context while examining their relevance in contemporary systems.

The introduction outlines the scope of the study, the methodological approach, and the rationale for analyzing Chanakya through multiple academic lenses. It also highlights the importance of revisiting classical political thought to understand modern governance challenges.

# Abstract

This multidisciplinary study examines the contributions of Chanakya (Kautilya) to economics, political science, sociology, governance, and statecraft. Drawing from the Arthashastra and related classical texts, the research explores Chanakya’s analytical frameworks, administrative models, and philosophical foundations. Comparative insights with Indian Acharyas and Greek thinkers highlight the universality and uniqueness of his ideas. The study also evaluates the relevance of Chanakya’s principles in modern leadership, public administration, and geopolitical strategy.
